2 — What is the problem?

Tap a problem to see common causes and basic troubleshooting. An online assessment is a starting point — it can never replace a professional diagnosis.

3 — Estimate the repair price

Estimates are indicative ranges in Moroccan dirhams (MAD). Real prices depend on parts quality (original vs compatible), parts availability, and the technician. We never display invented prices as verified — when we have no reliable data, we say so.

Missing a price? Technicians and shop owners can add price data (demo admin). Final quotes always come from the technician after inspection.

4 — Find a repair shop

Search by city and neighborhood, filter by brand and service. Listings are added by shop owners and are marked unverified until our team confirms them in person. We never invent shops, ratings, or phone numbers.

About Sla7ha

"Sla7ha" means "fix it" in Moroccan Darija. Sla7ha is an independent platform built to make phone repair in Morocco more transparent: clear problem descriptions, honest price expectations, and real shops you can contact directly. This first version runs entirely in your browser — no accounts, no tracking, no data uploaded.

Privacy

This version stores any data you enter (shop listings, price edits) only on your own device via localStorage. Nothing is transmitted to a server. When accounts and a backend launch, this policy will be updated before any data collection begins.

Terms & medical-style disclaimer

Problem guides and price ranges are informational only and may not match your situation. Diagnosis and quotes must come from a qualified technician. Use of the site is at your own risk; Sla7ha is not a party to any repair agreement between you and a shop.
